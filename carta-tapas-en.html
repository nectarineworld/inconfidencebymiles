Food for your events

Tapas that go with a great night.

An open menu of Mediterranean bites for cocktail parties, birthdays, group dinners and private celebrations. Pick your favourites or let us put together the selection that best suits your event.

10—130 guests By reservation Tailor-made selection

01 — Tostas & focaccias

Coca bread, focaccia and combinations designed to eat standing up, raise a glass and keep the night going.

Pastrami tosta

Toasted coca bread, mustard cream, beef pastrami, gherkin and lemon-pickled red onion.

Sobrasada, Brie and honey tosta

Toasted coca bread, sobrasada, lightly melted Brie and a touch of honey.

Chicken, parmesan and confit tomato tosta

Coca bread, smooth parmesan cream, marinated chicken, confit tomato and fresh rocket.

Smoked salmon tosta

Toasted coca bread, lemon and dill cream cheese, smoked salmon, capers and pickled red onion.

Escalivada, goat cheese and honey tosta

Toasted coca bread, homemade escalivada, goat cheese and a touch of honey. A Catalan classic in bite size.

Mediterranean mini focaccia

Artisan focaccia in rotating flavours: mortadella, pistachio cream and rocket; mozzarella, pesto and confit tomato; chicken and parmesan; sobrasada, Brie and honey; or roasted vegetables with whipped feta.

02 — Fresh & vegetarian

Colour, freshness and plant-based produce so everyone at the table finds their bite.

Caprese skewer

Cherry tomato, mozzarella, fresh basil, pesto and extra virgin olive oil.

Roasted aubergine and whipped feta bite

Roasted aubergine on crispy bread, whipped feta, honey and toasted walnuts.

Crudités with Mediterranean hummus

Fresh seasonal vegetables with homemade hummus: classic, beetroot and lemon, roasted pepper, or black olive and herbs.

Gastro-style devilled eggs

Yolk cream, mayonnaise and Dijon mustard, fresh chives and pickled red onion.

Ensaladilla rusa on a crispy rosquilla

Homemade Spanish potato salad on a crispy rosquilla or toast. With anchovy or vegetarian.

03 — Hot & crispy

The essentials of a good sharing table: bold, generous and easy to share.

Roquefort-stuffed mushrooms

Baked mushrooms with Roquefort and cream cheese, optionally finished with toasted walnut.

Our bravas

Crispy potatoes, mild smoked brava sauce, aioli and confit onion.

Crispy chicken fingers

Marinated chicken in a panko crust, served with honey mustard sauce.

Homemade Spanish omelette

Potato and onion tortilla, with or without chistorra sausage, served as bite-size pinchos.

Crispy dates

Choose between dates stuffed with Brie and walnut wrapped in puff pastry, or dates with crispy bacon.

Some dishes may vary depending on the season, availability and event format. We can recommend the most balanced combination for your number of guests.
